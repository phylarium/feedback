# Phylarium feedback

This is the place to report bugs and share ideas for [Phylarium](https://phylarium.com).
Every report gets read. Short reports are welcome. A one-line bug report is better than no report.

## Found a bug?

[**Report a bug**](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/issues/new?template=bug.yml)

Tell us what happened and what you expected. If you can, add:

- **Version.** Open the science book, tap **⚙**, and look under the world stats (for example `Phylarium 0.3.1`).
- **Device and browser.** For example "iPhone 15, Safari" or "Windows, Chrome".
- **A screenshot or video.** Drag it into the text box.

Before you post, take a quick look at the [open bugs](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/issues). If your bug is already there, add a 👍 or a comment instead.

## Have an idea or something to say?

[**Share an idea**](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/discussions/new?category=ideas) · [**Ask a question**](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/discussions/new?category=q-a) · [**General chat**](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/discussions/new?category=general)

Ideas, balance thoughts, "this part was confusing" and "I loved this" all go in [Discussions](https://github.com/phylarium/feedback/discussions).
Vote 👍 on the ideas you want most. Popular ideas get looked at first.

## What happens next

- Bugs get a label when they are confirmed, and close when the fix is live.
- Fixes reach the [beta](https://phylarium.com/beta/) first, then the main game.
- Good ideas move from Discussions to an issue when they are planned.

You need a free GitHub account to post.
